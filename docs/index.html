Generalized Hamming weights of projective Reed–Muller codes

Bounds and exact values for the generalized Hamming weights \(d_r(\mathrm{PRM}_q(d,m))\) of projective Reed–Muller codes. Choose \(q\) and \(m\): each block below is a code \(\mathrm{PRM}_q(d,m)\), and the entry in row \(i\) and column \(j\) is the weight \(d_{i+j}\). A single number is the exact value, and a–b means \(a \le d_r \le b\). Click on any entry to see where its bounds come from.

Where the bounds come from

  • Exact values. The complete hierarchy for \(d < q\) [SJ26, Thm. 1.1] (conjectured by Beelen, Datta and Ghorpade [BDG22]), for \((m-1)(q-1) < d < m(q-1)\) [SJ26, Cor. 4.2], and for \(d \ge m(q-1)\); the minimum distance [Sør91]; and the first weights for the degrees of [SJ26, Prop. 6.12].
  • Upper bound. The bound \(B_r(q,d,m)\) of [SJ26, Thm. 6.7], valid in every degree. It is sharp for \(d < q\), and [SJ26, Conj. 6.9] states that it is always sharp.
  • Lower bound. The recursive bound of [SJ24, Thm. 7], applied with the best bounds known for \(\mathrm{PRM}_q(d,m-1)\) and \(\mathrm{PRM}_q(d-q+1,m-1)\).
  • Duality. Wei duality [Wei91; SJ24, Thm. 9] between \(\mathrm{PRM}_q(d,m)\) and its dual, which is \(\mathrm{PRM}_q(m(q-1)-d,m)\), plus the all-ones vector when \(q-1\) divides \(d\) [Sør91], as in [SJ24, Section V-C].
  • Classical bounds. Two bounds valid for every linear code, used in both directions: the bound \((q^r-q)\,d_r \ge (q^r-1)\,d_{r-1}\) [HKLY95, Thm. 1] ([HKY92, Thm. 1] for \(q = 2\)), which implies the strict monotonicity \(d_{r-1} < d_r\) [Wei91, Thm. 1; SJ24, Thm. 5], and the bound \(n - d_r \ge \sum_{i=0}^{k-r-1} \lceil (d_{r+1}-d_r)/q^i \rceil\) [HKLY95, proof of Thm. 4], which contains the Griesmer bound. Together they contain the other bounds of [Wei91], [HKY92] and [HKLY95] that can be evaluated from bounds for the weights, such as the generalized Singleton, Plotkin-type and Griesmer-type bounds.
  • Computed values. Values computed with the Sage package GHWs [SJ25], with their source.
